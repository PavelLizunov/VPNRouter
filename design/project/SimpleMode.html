Calm — what changed vs. the current Claude draft

Red destructive CTA → Arctic accent. Small dot instead of a giant green circle — the card is the signal, not a billboard.

Config is now a visible row with key·value (was an empty dropdown placeholder).

Header shrunk to 32px logo + name + menu. Badges kept but shrunk to 8px pill chips under the name.

Orb — the emotional one

The glacier orb holds the penguin — colour + inner shadow = state. No text label needed to read the screen.

Connecting pulses softly (not a jittery spinner). Off looks cold-grey, alive but resting.

Works as a mascot without turning into a cartoon. Same CTA + config + advanced structure underneath.

Hero — for power users who skim

One word carries the screen. Colour-gradient wordmark for "Protected", muted type for "Off".

A 2×2 mono grid below gives you the four facts that matter: server, IP, uptime, config. No fluff.

Most information-dense of the three, but still one-glance readable.
