Voice

Do

  • Plain, specific, technically honest. "TCP+TLS verified, 48 ms" — not "Ultra-fast server".
  • Name real things. sing-box, VLESS, Reality, Zapret — users chose this tool for them.
  • Explain the "why". "Skip Russian traffic so bank sites don't break" beats "Enable bypass".
  • Short buttons. Connect · Apply · Deep verify.

Don't

  • No marketing puffery. Never "military-grade encryption" or "lightning-fast".
  • No emoji flood. Emoji only where a glyph outperforms a word (flags, signal dots).
  • No generic stock icons. Use the penguin; placeholder before fake.
  • No bright pure blue #2563EB — we're Arctic, not Facebook.
